03 · Market Problem Statement

The Packaging & Trust Execution Gap

Most UK SMEs are actively trying to source eco-friendly, secure packaging, yet remain stuck in a cycle of trial-and-error procurement — lacking access to packaging engineers, sustainability specialists, or enterprise-grade brand protection.

Three Biggest Sources of Packaging Friction

Manual Catalogue Searches

Trial-and-error procurement replaces structured, data-driven material selection — repeated for every new SKU.

No Structured Specifications

A lack of structural physics testing or engineering logic leaves brands unable to verify a material will protect a fragile product.

No Affordable Anti-Counterfeit Tools

SMEs cannot afford enterprise-grade brand-protection analytics, leaving them exposed to grey-market diversion and tampering.

Regulatory & Financial Pressure

The Plastic Packaging Tax and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) frameworks now require UK businesses to evidence material sustainability and absorb end-of-life management costs. Most brands view sustainable packaging as a growth driver but lack the "smart infrastructure" to convert raw product dimensions into compliant, cost-efficient designs — leaving a widening gap between basic online box wholesalers and overly complex enterprise brand-protection platforms.

04 · The Proposed Solution

A Closed-Loop "PackOps" Lifecycle

PackSure AI connects brand packaging choices, material-structural integrity and regulatory compliance into one continuous, self-learning loop — not a one-time procurement search.

STEP 1 · INTENT

Intent-to-Packaging Auto-Synthesis

A business owner inputs raw product specs — e.g. a fragile wellness supplement needing high-barrier compostable protection under EPR — and the engine deconstructs it into an optimised packaging spec.

STEP 2 · CLARIFY

Real-Time Regulatory Clarification

Instant clarification on material-specific tariffs (£217.85/tonne plastic tax) and eco-modulation RAG scores, stripping away compliance complexity.

STEP 3 · MONITOR

Transit Failure & Refusal Forensics Engine

If a parcel is damaged, the engine analyses carrier transit metrics and return logs, compiling an automated customer refusal summary — seal degradation, compression, or humidity drift.

STEP 4 · PROTECT

Scan-Based Trust Ledger

Serialised, tamper-evident QR/NFC identities monitor verification behaviour in real time, identifying counterfeit hotspots and grey-market diversion.

Worked example

A premium organic skincare brand develops a circular fibre composite for a delicate glass serum bottle that consistently survives shipping with a zero-damage rate and 100% recyclability. PackSure AI "fingerprints" that structural logic, letting product and logistics teams replicate the compliant, high-performing design across new SKUs instantly — eliminating the "logic loss" that occurs during staff or supplier transitions.
